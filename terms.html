You may post only properties you are authorized to advertise. Listing details, rent, charging period, photos, location, and availability must be truthful and kept up to date. Three photos are included with a new listing; adding a fourth photo requires the displayed fee, and the total limit is four photos. 3. Review, visibility, and expiry

Listings may require administrator approval before becoming public. KejaSasa may pause, reject, remove, or request changes to a listing that appears inaccurate or violates these terms. Listings remain visible after the initial 14-day free contact period, provided they are approved and available; administrator moderation may still pause or remove them.

4. Free listings and contact unlock payments

Each account can publish five free listings by default; the administrator may change this limit. Call and WhatsApp contact details are available free for the configured contact period (14 days by default); afterward, visitors may pay the displayed small KejaSasa system fee to unlock a contact. The unlock fee is paid to KejaSasa, not the landlord or house owner. A paid feature is applied only after payment is confirmed. Do not submit payment if the displayed amount or details are incorrect.

5. Contact information and safety

Contact details may be visible to visitors during the free contact period or after they unlock them. Do not include information you do not want published. KejaSasa helps people discover listings but does not verify ownership, property condition, availability, or the accuracy of every claim. Confirm details directly with the owner or agent and use your own judgment before paying or signing an agreement.

6. Service availability and changes

Features may change or be temporarily unavailable. KejaSasa may update these terms as the service changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised terms.

7. Questions or reports

Use the contact or report options on KejaSasa to ask a question or notify the administrators about a listing.
