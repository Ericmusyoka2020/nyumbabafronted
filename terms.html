Terms & Conditions

2. Property listings

You may post only properties you are authorized to advertise. Listing details, rent, charging period, photos, location, and availability must be truthful and kept up to date. Three photos are included with a new listing; adding a fourth photo requires the displayed fee, and the total limit is four photos. Do not post misleading, fraudulent, unlawful, or discriminatory content.

3. Review, visibility, and expiry

Listings may require administrator approval before becoming public. Lodora may pause, reject, remove, or request changes to a listing that appears inaccurate or violates these terms. Listings remain visible after the initial free contact period, provided they are approved and available; administrator moderation may still pause or remove them.

4. Free listings and contact unlock payments

Each account can publish five free listings by default; the administrator may change this limit. Call, WhatsApp, and exact location details are available free for the configured contact period (14 days by default); afterward, visitors may pay the displayed small Lodora system fee to unlock them. The unlock fee is paid to Lodora, not the landlord or house owner. An account is not required; enter a mobile number to receive the payment prompt. A paid feature is applied only after payment is confirmed. Do not submit payment if the displayed amount or details are incorrect.

5. Contact information and safety

Contact details may be visible to visitors during the free contact period or after they unlock them. Lodora records calls, WhatsApp-link clicks, and location-pin requests with the associated listing and owner/agent for administration and support. Account name and phone may be shown in these records if signed in. Do not include information you do not want published. Lodora helps people discover listings but does not verify ownership, property condition, availability, or the accuracy of every claim. Confirm details directly with the owner or agent and use your own judgment before paying or signing an agreement.

6. Service availability and changes

Features may change or be temporarily unavailable. Lodora may update these terms as the service changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised terms.

7. Questions or reports

Use the contact or report options on Lodora to ask a question or notify the administrators about a listing.
