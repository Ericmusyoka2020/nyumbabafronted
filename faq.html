Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a building or listing?

Use the search box to search by building name, town, estate, or county. You can also filter by property type and rent.

Which kinds of properties can be listed?

Listings include rooms, bedsitters, studios, apartments, houses, lodging, cottages, and restaurants. A poster can select the types available in one building.

How should I enter the rent?

Enter one price in Kenyan shillings and select whether it is charged per day, night, month, or year.

Why is my new listing not visible?

House and home advertisements are free. New listings are submitted for administrator approval and remain visible in public search while available.

How long does a listing stay active?

Each account can publish 5 free listings by default; the administrator can change this limit. Listings stay visible. Call and WhatsApp contacts are free to view for the first 14 days by default, then visitors can unlock a number for the small amount shown. The administrator sets this period and the unlock fee. The fee goes to the KejaSasa system, not the landlord or house owner.

How many photos can I add?

A new listing includes 3 photos. You can pay the displayed admin-set fee to add a fourth photo. Listings allow up to 4 photos total.

Can I promote a listing?

Available priority plans are shown in My Listings. An administrator can also assign a custom priority duration.

How do I contact a property owner?

Use the Call or WhatsApp links shown on the listing. If contacts are locked, choose Unlock contact and follow the payment prompt; the small fee goes to the KejaSasa system, not the landlord or house owner. Confirm the rent, availability, location, and other terms directly with the owner or agent.

How do I report a listing?

Open the listing details, choose a report reason, and send it to the administrators for review.
