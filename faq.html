Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a building or listing?

Use the search box to search by building name, town, estate, or county. You can also filter by property type and rent.

Which kinds of properties can be listed?

Listings include rooms, bedsitters, studios, apartments, houses, lodging, and cottages. A poster can select the types available in one building.

How should I enter the rent?

Enter one price in Kenyan shillings and select whether it is charged per day, night, month, or year.

Why is my new listing not visible?

New listings are submitted for administrator approval. They appear in public search after approval and while they remain available and unexpired.

How long does a listing stay active?

The post duration depends on the free-post rules or plan applied to the listing. Check My Listings for the expiry date and remaining time. An administrator may gift additional listing days.

Can I promote a listing?

Available priority plans are shown in My Listings. An administrator can also assign a custom priority duration.

How do I contact a property owner?

Use the Call or WhatsApp links shown on the listing. Confirm the rent, availability, location, and any other terms directly with the owner or agent before making arrangements.

How do I report a listing?

Open the listing details, choose a report reason, and send it to the administrators for review.
